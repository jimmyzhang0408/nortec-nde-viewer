# Sample data

`surface-cracks-eca-single-surf25-2026-07-13T04-54-27.nde` is an encoded,
32-channel ECA scan supplied and authorized for redistribution by the project
owner. It is included solely to demonstrate and verify the viewer.

The file reports NDE Open Format 4.3.0 metadata and contains 1,416 mapped scan
positions across 32 channels. The viewer reports any processing approximations
in its **Processing notes** tab.

The sample is not a reference standard, calibration artifact, or acceptance
criterion. Do not use its displayed indications to validate an inspection
procedure or instrument performance.
